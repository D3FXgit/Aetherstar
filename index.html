Karaoke night, scored.

AetherStar is free party karaoke for Windows and Mac. It plays UltraStar songs, follows every singer's pitch and scores each of you out of 10,000.

Download See what it does

What you need

A computer

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, or a Mac with Apple silicon.

Download
Microphones

Any mic your computer sees: USB mics, a headset, or the inputs of an audio interface, one per singer. Up to eight.

Songs

Songs in the UltraStar format, which fans have charted songs in for years. AetherStar doesn't come with any.

What's in it

All features
  • SingStar-style scoringUp to 10,000 points per singer, a verdict on every line, and a rank from Tone Deaf to Superstar.
  • Party, Versus, DuetLet the app draw singers and mystery songs, duel one on one, or split a duet.
  • A second screenLyrics and a live scoreboard on the TV, menus on the laptop.
  • Fair timingA short calibration measures speaker and mic delay, so Bluetooth speakers don't cost points.
  • JukeboxPlays your library with its videos when nobody's singing.
  • Your way to playKeyboard, mouse or gamepad. English and German menus, more with one text file.

Where it comes from

AetherStar exists because of SingStar, the PlayStation karaoke series Sony launched in 2004. It turned singing into a scored party game, and its scoring and rank ladder are what AetherStar plays like.

The songs come from the fans. UltraStar Deluxe and the UltraStar projects before it created the open song format AetherStar reads, and the USDB community has charted songs in it for years.

AetherStar is an independent project with no connection to Sony or to those projects. It shares no code or assets with SingStar.
