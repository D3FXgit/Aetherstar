Privacy notice

This website presents AetherStar, a free open-source karaoke game. It is a private, non-commercial hobby project by D3FXgit: no ads, no paid content, no accounts, no donations.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub's servers process the technical data your browser sends, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

No cookies, no tracking

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. There are no analytics, ads, social media plugins or embedded third-party content. The font is served from the same place as the site.

Downloads and links

Downloads and the source code are on GitHub. Links to GitHub, USDB, Wikipedia and other sites take you to those sites, and their privacy policies apply there. Nothing is sent to them before you click.

Contact and your rights

Questions about this site, including requests about your data under the GDPR, can go to the project's issue tracker on GitHub. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Keine Cookies, kein Tracking

Die Website setzt keine Cookies und speichert nichts auf Ihrem Gerät. Es gibt keine Analyse- oder Werbedienste, keine Social-Media-Plugins und keine eingebetteten Inhalte Dritter. Die Schrift wird vom selben Ort geladen wie die Website.
